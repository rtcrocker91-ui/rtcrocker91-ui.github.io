About the studio

Dancing Tomato Studio was founded by Haley Crocker — a Portland, Maine-based artist, designer, and maker with over 15 years of experience working across disciplines.

The work spans illustration, graphic design, and ceramics, connected by a commitment to craft, place, and making things that are genuinely worth keeping. The work is always original — conceived, made, and finished by hand.
